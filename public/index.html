Privacy & data

Messages, photos and voice notes sent through Hotel Ping are stored securely in the hotel's database as part of normal business records. The General Manager and other admins can open any conversation at any time, including for training, coordination and resolving disputes. Please keep messages professional, and avoid sharing personal financial or medical details through this system.
